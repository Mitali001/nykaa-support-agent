Routine policy questions and standard tracking explanations can be handled by the automated support agent when the knowledge base provides a clear answer. Orders with a high escalation score, a delivery delay beyond the expected window, a disputed payment, or a damaged-item claim requiring human review should be referred to the appropriate support queue. Suspected account compromise, requests involving sensitive payment credentials, and safety concerns require immediate human handling. The agent should explain the reason for escalation without exposing internal risk scores unnecessarily.
